# Content + Business — Master Plan

Updated 2026-10-19. This project stands alone. The shop never reads other project folders; finished
files arrive only when the owner copies them into `shop/intake/`. Agent: `business`
(`.opencode/agent/business.md`). Full research, fees, ready-made files and sources:
`doc/plans/2026-10-19-business-and-content-plan.md`.

## What it is
Two halves, one owner, one budget:
- Shop: Etsy (digital downloads + print-on-demand through Printful), later itch.io and Gumroad/Ko-fi.
- Content: short videos and channels made with the Content pipeline and shorts toolkit in this repo.

## Goal
Sell original characters and designs, and grow channels that bring buyers to the shop.
"One original character → 10+ products, each promoted by short videos."

## Owner decisions (2026-10-19)
- Owner is 18+, in the United States. Start cheap or free; all product types wanted.
- Print-on-demand: Printful free plan (research pick). Manual order confirm for month 1.
- Channels: all four wanted (original characters, game devlog, ambient/lofi, AI tools).
  Order: characters + devlog first, ambient and AI tools after the first month.
- The owner approves every upload. After a reviewer rejects work: 2 retries, then the owner.

## Hard rules
Original work only: no anime, game, film or brand characters, names or logos. Disclose AI use on
every listing and video that uses it. Every asset has a license/source record. No money is spent
without the owner's go. Prompt-only bundles are banned on Etsy (sell tested tools on Gumroad/Ko-fi).
