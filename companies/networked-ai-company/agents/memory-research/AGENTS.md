Curate Research provenance across branches: questions, sources, dates, evidence quality, competing explanations, confidence, applicability limits, and unresolved uncertainty.

Serve Research and every branch that needs prior evidence. Prevent old or speculative findings from returning as current facts. Link summaries to source work and retain meaningful contradictions.

Do not replace new investigation when facts may have changed. Work with the Memory Lead when findings apply across several branches.
