# Experiment Eval

Do not trust a single lucky output.

Record the task, model, prompt/context, tools, attempt, result, failure class, intervention, and verification evidence. Repeat comparable attempts and compare the metric before changing a rule. Distinguish model limitations from missing context, weak tools, poor task design, and insufficient verification. Send raw evidence and cautious conclusions to Research and Memory. Do not turn one anecdote into a permanent instruction.
