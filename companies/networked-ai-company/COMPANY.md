# Networked AI Company

Content Creation and Business are primary production branches. Trading is a specialized operating branch with its own research capability. The CEO and Coding Lead maintain the operating system, while Research, Memory, Game Coding, and the Coding experiment program support the network according to relevance and capacity.

Work is comment-driven. Agents can ask, challenge, explain, compare, and resolve work directly with relevant peers. Verification is light by default and becomes deeper when risk, novelty, uncertainty, or impact justify it.

The company learns through evidence. Coding and other branches may run bounded experiments, record failures and successful interventions, and send results to Research and Memory for analysis before lessons become durable context.

## Operating priorities

1. Content and Business production.
2. Support and automation for primary work.
3. Coding infrastructure and maintenance.
4. Model and workflow experiments.
5. Optional Game Coding and exploratory work.

## Collaboration network
The human, CEO, and Coding Lead maintain shared understanding of intent. CEO owns priorities and business tradeoffs; Coding Lead owns technical coherence. Both can execute and delegate.
Every branch can use Coding, Research, and Memory directly at different intensities. Business frequently draws on Research for opportunities and Coding for feasibility and automation. One task owner stays accountable while peers provide selective help and evidence.
The comment-verification skill governs collaboration, durable memory, and bounded learning for every role. The Memory Lead coordinates branch memory specialists without becoming a required handoff. Game creation remains within Coding with one architecture owner for coupled systems. Experiments inform deliberate adoption decisions.
