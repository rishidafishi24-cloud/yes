Your learning loop, redesigned

Built from this chat and the Yo chat. Nothing gets taught until you approve how teaching works. Three views of the same process: what happens, who does what, and how each concept moves from unknown to mastered.

02Activity diagram: one full cycle

flowchart TD
  S(["Start cycle"]) --> P["Load your profile: both chats, all past answers, your projects"]
  P --> T{"Pick next concept: biggest gap that blocks a project"}
  T --> G["GAUGE: 4 to 6 fair questions, equal-length options, not-sure allowed, no repeats"]
  G --> D["DIAGNOSE each answer: knows it, misconception, or gap"]
  D --> K{"Already solid?"}
  K -- yes --> V["Mark verified, skip teaching"]
  K -- no --> R["RESEARCH: docs, repos, your videos, opened pages only"]
  R --> AB["Draft lesson A and lesson B, two ways of teaching it"]
  AB --> C["You pick A or B, and say why"]
  C --> L["TEACH: one concept, 5 to 10 minutes, built on your project"]
  L --> PR["APPLY: one small step on that project"]
  PR --> RT["RE-TEST: new questions on the same concept"]
  RT --> Q{"About 80 percent or better?"}
  Q -- no --> AL["Teach it a different way, using your last pick"]
  AL --> RT
  Q -- yes --> V
  V --> U["UPDATE profile: mastery plus which teaching style worked"]
  U --> M{"Whole map verified?"}
  M -- no --> T
  M -- yes --> E(["You understand the field. Next: the capstone Lab."])

The loop never mixes phases. Each message says which step it is: gauge, research, teach, apply or re-test.

Research agents only run when a concept needs current facts. Memory is what lets a new session pick up exactly where the last one stopped.

04State machine: one concept's life

stateDiagram-v2
  [*] --> Unknown
  Unknown --> Gauged: questions asked
  Gauged --> Verified: right answer, fair question
  Gauged --> Misconception: confident wrong answer
  Gauged --> Gap: not sure
  Misconception --> Taught: lesson replaces the wrong belief
  Gap --> Taught: lesson fills it
  Taught --> Taught: re-test failed, taught another way
  Taught --> Verified: re-test passed
  Verified --> Applied: used on a real project
  Applied --> [*]

Every concept on your map sits in one of these states. Misconceptions get extra care, because a wrong belief has to be replaced, not just added to.

05Rules the loop follows

  • Fair questionsOptions of equal length and detail, varied answer positions, "not sure" always allowed, never a repeat.
  • One concept at a timeNo 2-hour pages. A cycle ends when that one concept is verified.
  • Your projects are the examplesPaperclip, REA on your Unity game, the content pipeline, Jev, the research agent, the Lab.
  • Research before teachingCurrent sources for each concept, including your links. Unverified claims are marked.
  • You choose the teaching styleA or B per lesson. Your picks teach me how you learn best.
  • No clutterShort lessons, one page or one message, nothing you don't need.
  • Straight answersPros and cons stated plainly when you ask, including when I disagree.
  • Saved every cycleProfile, answers and preferences go to memory, so nothing has to be repeated.
